    Try it

    Plays a chord, or a short riff for the acid bass. The KeyStep mod strip opens the filter live on every sound.

    Lesson sounds

    Each lesson picks a sound that suits it: electric piano for scales, pad for chord changes, stab or organ for garage stabs. Pick a patch above to use your own on every lesson. Ear training always uses a plain sound.

    Patches are approximations in the style of each synth, not the real instruments or official sounds. Knob settings are saved on this device.

    Timing

    Tap any key along with 12 clicks. The app measures the delay of this device's speaker and MIDI and corrects for it.

    Options

    Wiring, as verified

    • KeyStep MIDI Out to Circuit MIDI In (5-pin).
    • KeyStep USB-C to this device (MacBook or Pixel) for power and MIDI into the app.
    • Circuit defaults: Synth 1 = ch 1, Synth 2 = ch 2, MIDI 1 = ch 3, MIDI 2 = ch 4, drums = ch 10.
    • Circuit clock into the app: Circuit USB to the MacBook too, then pick it under Clock from. On the Pixel the KeyStep uses the only port, so use the app loop.
    • Laptop sound: turn the UMC MIX knob towards the middle, or you will not hear the app.
    • Later (week 5): Circuit Thru set to copy Out, to KeyStep MIDI In for clock; KeyStep CV Pitch and Gate to the Crave.
